mₚ/mₑ · proton-electron mass ratio

양성자-전자 질량비

Takeaway
  • The relative standard uncertainty fell by a factor of about 343,751 from the 1969 to the 2022 edition (6.0×10⁻⁶ → 1.7×10⁻¹¹): the error bars narrowed that much.
  • Of 9 earlier editions with a measured value, 6 have the 2022 value outside ±1σ (the error bar) and 2 outside ±2σ (the bar doubled); the normal expectation is about 1 in 3 and 1 in 22.
  • The largest move between editions came in 1969→1973, at 3.9 times the combined standard uncertainty of the two editions (|z| = 3.9).
