μp/μN · proton magnetic moment to nuclear magneton ratio

양성자 자기 모멘트(핵마그네톤 단위)

Takeaway
  • The relative standard uncertainty fell by a factor of about 20,732 from the 1969 to the 2022 edition (6.1×10⁻⁶ → 2.9×10⁻¹⁰): the error bars narrowed that much.
  • Of 9 earlier editions with a measured value, 2 have the 2022 value outside ±1σ (the error bar) and 1 outside ±2σ (the bar doubled); the normal expectation is about 1 in 3 and 1 in 22.
  • The largest move between editions came in 1969→1973, at 3.7 times the combined standard uncertainty of the two editions (|z| = 3.7).
