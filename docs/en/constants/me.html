me · electron mass

전자 질량

Takeaway
  • The relative standard uncertainty fell by a factor of about 19,285 from the 1969 to the 2022 edition (5.9×10⁻⁶ → 3.1×10⁻¹⁰): the error bars narrowed that much.
  • Of 9 earlier editions with a measured value, 8 have the 2022 value outside ±1σ (the error bar) and 6 outside ±2σ (the bar doubled); the normal expectation is about 1 in 3 and 1 in 22.
  • The largest move between editions came in 2018→2022, at 3.1 times the combined standard uncertainty of the two editions (|z| = 3.1).
