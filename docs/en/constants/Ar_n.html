Ar(n) · neutron relative atomic mass

중성자의 상대 원자 질량

Takeaway
  • The relative standard uncertainty fell about 250-fold from the 1969 to the 2022 edition (9.9×10⁻⁸ → 4.0×10⁻¹⁰): the error bars narrowed that much.
  • Of 9 earlier editions with a measured value, 2 have the 2022 value outside ±1σ (the error bar) and 2 outside ±2σ (the bar doubled); the normal expectation is about 1 in 3 and 1 in 20.
  • The largest move between editions was 1973→1986, 2.7 times the combined standard uncertainty of the two editions (|z| = 2.7).
