e · elementary charge

기본 전하

Takeaway
  • The relative standard uncertainty fell about 714-fold from the 1969 to the 2014 edition (4.4×10⁻⁶ → 6.1×10⁻⁹): the error bars narrowed that much.
  • Of 8 earlier editions with a measured value, 7 have the 2022 value outside ±1σ (the error bar) and 4 outside ±2σ (the bar doubled); the normal expectation is about 1 in 3 and 1 in 20 (the 2022 value is exact).
  • The largest move between editions was 1973→1986, 2.6 times the combined standard uncertainty of the two editions (|z| = 2.6).
  • Exact from the 2018 edition (fixed by definition rather than measured), so its uncertainty is zero.
