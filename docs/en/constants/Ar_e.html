Takeaway
  • From 1969 to 2022 the relative uncertainty fell from 6.2×10⁻⁶ to 1.8×10⁻¹¹, about 350,509-fold.
  • Of 9 earlier editions with a measured value, 8 missed the 2022 value at ±1σ and 1 at ±2σ (normal: about 32% and 5%).
  • The largest move between editions was 1969→1973, 3.7 times the combined uncertainty of the two editions.
