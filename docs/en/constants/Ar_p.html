Core constant

Ar(p) · proton relative atomic mass

양성자의 상대 원자 질량

Takeaway
  • The relative standard uncertainty fell by a factor of about 9,639 from the 1969 to the 2022 edition (7.9×10⁻⁸ → 8.2×10⁻¹²): the error bars narrowed that much.
  • Of 9 earlier editions with a measured value, 6 have the 2022 value outside ±1σ (the error bar) and 4 outside ±2σ (the bar doubled); the normal expectation is about 1 in 3 and 1 in 22.
  • The largest move between editions came in 2014→2018, at 2.4 times the combined standard uncertainty of the two editions (|z| = 2.4).
Unit
none (dimensionless)
