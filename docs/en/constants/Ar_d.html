Takeaway
  • From 1986 to 2022 the relative uncertainty fell from 1.2×10⁻⁸ to 7.4×10⁻¹², about 1,600-fold.
  • Of 7 earlier editions with a measured value, 4 missed the 2022 value at ±1σ and 4 at ±2σ (normal: about 32% and 5%).
  • The largest move between editions was 2018→2022, 4.7 times the combined uncertainty of the two editions.
